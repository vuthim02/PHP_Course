About Me

Hey there!

I'm Alex, a full-stack web developer based in Austin, Texas. I've been building things on the web for over six years, starting with simple HTML pages in a college dorm room and progressing to distributed microservices handling millions of requests a day.

I believe the web should be open, accessible, and fast. Every project I work on — whether it's a CLI tool, a REST API, or a marketing site — gets my full attention to performance, usability, and clean code.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me hiking in the Texas hill country, reading sci-fi, or contributing to open-source projects.

My Story

My journey began with a "View Page Source" right-click on a website. That single moment sparked a curiosity that led me through HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, databases, servers, and eventually a degree in Computer Science. Along the way I've worked at startups, agencies, and as a freelance contractor.

Philosophy

  • Simple is better. Solve the problem, not the abstraction.
  • Accessibility is not optional. If the web isn't for everyone, it's not working.
  • Shipping beats perfecting. Done is better than perfect, but done well is best.
